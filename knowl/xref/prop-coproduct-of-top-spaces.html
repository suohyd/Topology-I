Proposition 1.2.4.

Let \(I\) be a nonempty set, and let \((X_\alpha,\tau_\alpha)\) be a topological space for each \(\alpha\in I\text{.}\) The following topologies on \(\coprod X_\alpha\) (see Definition 1.1.7) are equivalent.
  1. The topology \(\tau_1\) such that \(U\subseteq\coprod X_\alpha\) is open if and only if \(U=\coprod U_\alpha\) for some \(U_\alpha\subseteq X_\alpha\) open.
  2. The topology \(\tau_2\) such that it is the finest topology on \(\coprod X_\alpha\) such that inclusion maps \(i_\alpha\colon X_\alpha\to\coprod X_\alpha\) are continuous.
  3. (Universal property of the coproduct topology). The topology \(\tau_3\) satisfying the following universal property.
    For every topological space \(Z\) and every function \(f\colon\coprod X_\alpha\to Z\text{,}\) \(f\) is continuous if and only if for each \(\alpha\in I\text{,}\) \(f\circ i_\alpha\colon X_\alpha\to Z\) is continuous, where \(i_\alpha\colon X_\alpha\to\coprod X_\alpha\) is natural inclusion.
