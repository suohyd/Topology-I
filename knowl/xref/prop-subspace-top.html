Proposition 1.3.1.

Let \((X,\tau)\) a topological space and let \(Y\) be a subset of \(X\text{.}\) Following topologies defined on \(Y\) are all equivalent.
  1. The topology \(\tau_1=\{U\cap X:U\text{ an open subset of }X\}\text{.}\)
  2. The coarsest topology \(\tau_2\) on \(Y\) for which the canonical inclusion \(i\colon Y\hookrightarrow X\) is continuous.
  3. (Universal property of subspace topology). The topology \(\tau_3\) on \(Y\) satisfying the following property.
    For every topological space \((Z,\tau_Z)\) and any function \(f\colon Z\to Y\text{,}\) \(f\) is continuous if and only if \(i\circ f\colon Z\to X\) is continuous. See the following diagram.
